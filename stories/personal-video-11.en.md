One of Ashkan’s early After Effects edits—and I got to be in it. ❤️🎬

My friend and collaborator Ashkan Mohammad Alipour had only recently started using After Effects and had not yet bought his computer system. I suggested he make an edit of me, recorded a voice message and gave it to him. This video is one of his first edits with After Effects. I remember his strong language skills helping him get comfortable with the program quickly.

Compared with his knowledge and edits today, this early work looks very rough to me. Even so, I am proud that I was part of the beginning and that he started making these early edits with me as the subject. My creative brief was wonderfully straightforward: “Come on, make an edit of me!” 😎

In my opinion, Ashkan is now one of the best editors in the world and one of the best collaborators anyone could have. That is my personal appreciation of my friend and the person I work with.
